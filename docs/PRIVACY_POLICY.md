## 1. Who we are
GIGGO ("we", "us") operates a marketplace connecting customers with local service
providers. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## 2. Information we collect
- **Account:** name, email, phone number, role (customer/provider), password (stored only as a hash).
- **Bookings:** the service, schedule, address/notes and contact details you provide for a job.
- **Location:** during an active job, a provider's device location is shared to
  power live tracking and ETA — **only after opt-in consent**, and only for that job.
- **Reviews:** ratings and review text you submit (analysed to summarise sentiment).
- **Provider verification (KYC):** document type, document number and any image you submit.
- **Payments:** processed by our payment provider; we store payment status and
  amounts, **not** full card details.
- **Technical:** device/app info and diagnostics for reliability and security.

## 3. How we use it
To provide and improve the service — matching, discovery and recommendations,
booking and job lifecycle, live tracking, ratings and fair ranking, payments and
escrow, notifications, fraud prevention and support, and to meet legal obligations.

## 4. Sharing
- **Between the parties to a booking:** a provider sees the contact/location
  details needed to perform the job, and vice-versa.
- **Service providers (processors):** payment gateway (e.g. PayHere), push
  notifications (e.g. Firebase), maps/ETA, error monitoring and hosting — each
  only for its function.
- **Legal:** where required by law. We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 5. Retention
We keep personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed for the
purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymise it.

## 6. Your rights
You may access or correct your information, request deletion of your account and
data, and withdraw location consent at any time (which stops live location
sharing). Contact [privacy@your-domain.com].

## 7. Security
We use encryption in transit, hashed passwords, authenticated APIs and least-
privilege access. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

## 8. Children
GIGGO is not intended for anyone under 18.

## 9. Changes
We may update this policy; material changes will be notified in-app or by email.
