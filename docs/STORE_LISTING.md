## Full description
> **GIGGO connects you with trusted local service professionals** — plumbers,
> electricians, cleaners, movers and more — and makes every step simple and safe.
>
> **For customers**
> • Discover pros by category, with fair ratings and honest, AI-summarised reviews
> • Get personalised recommendations picked for you
> • See a transparent price before you book — base, work and travel, no surprises
> • Track your provider live with an arrival ETA
> • Pay securely through escrow — funds are released only when you're happy
> • Rate and review in English or Sinhala/Singlish
>
> **For providers**
> • Manage job requests end to end — accept, travel, start, complete
> • Get verified to earn trust and more bookings
> • Grow with fair, quality-based ranking
>
> Plus a **Knowledge Hub** of tips and a **Tool Marketplace** for professionals.
>
> GIGGO — smart, fair, and built for Sri Lanka.
